## Role
The repository provides an AI-assisted workflow for turning technical IP into commercialization briefs, including analysis, screening valuation, licensing paths, and buyer categories.

The included deterministic fallback is a product behavior, not constitutional governance.

## Boundary
Commercial outputs must not be represented as formal appraisal, buyer commitment, legal approval, or Core/runtime authority without appropriate external verification.

Packaged PDFs, ZIPs, and prior proofs are historical/commercial artifacts unless explicitly promoted.

FINAL RULE: package and commercialize downstream; do not redefine upstream authority.
